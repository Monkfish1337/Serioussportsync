Two small additions to 1.4.0, both from Discussions.

## WWE NXT shows today's episode

TheSportsDB adds an NXT episode only after it has aired, so on the day of the
show there was no event, and the releases posted that night had nothing to
match. SSS now adds the episode itself on its usual night (NXT on Tuesday, Raw
on Monday, SmackDown on Friday) and swaps it for TheSportsDB's listing once it
appears. Thanks to everyone in
[Discussions #59](https://github.com/Monkfish1337/Serioussportsync/discussions/59).

## Reorder Nuvio collection folders

**Collections** (and **Configure → Collections**) has a new **Folder order**
list. Drag a folder, or use its arrow buttons on a phone; the order saves as
you go and Nuvio shows the folders that way. No more editing the JSON by hand.
Requested in [Discussions #116](https://github.com/Monkfish1337/Serioussportsync/discussions/116).
