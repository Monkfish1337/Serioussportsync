A small follow-up to 1.2.0 for **TorBox Usenet**.

## Choose where TorBox Usenet searches

The TorBox Usenet settings page now offers three separate sources:

- **Newznab indexers.** Add your indexers directly, as many as ten (for example
  NZBGeek and DrunkenSlug). SSS searches them all at once, and one being down
  doesn't stop the others.
- **NZBHydra.** Point it at your NZBHydra and let it search your indexers.
- **Prowlarr.** Point it at your Prowlarr and let it do the same.

**Test search** now reports each source separately, so you can see which
indexer is working. If you set up a single indexer in 1.2.0, it carries over
as your first Newznab indexer, so there's nothing to redo.

Your indexer API keys are encrypted like your other keys, and TorBox still
only ever receives the NZB file.
