form works there -- the same rule seen from the other side.

The prefix-free "<teams> <date>" form is the robust one and now goes out
first. This matters out of all proportion to its size: a slow provider gets a
bounded list and may reach only its first query before the budget expires, so
the order decides whether it finds anything. In the last log Prowlarr managed
exactly one query, and it was the prefixed one.
