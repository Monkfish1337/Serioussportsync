Fix the alignment on Promotions and Collections

## The Promotions table

A table cell inherits vertical-align: middle from its table, so every row's
one-line cells -- the kind badge, the poster shape, the catalog count -- floated
to the vertical centre of rows whose other cells ran to two or three lines.
Nothing shared a baseline. A badge was worse still: an inline-block sits its
bottom margin edge on the text baseline, so it hung visibly below the name
beside it. The Events column added in the previous commit made it worse by
adding a third line to most rows.

Top-aligned now, which needed saying twice: dropping Tabler's .table-vcenter
leaves the inherited middle in place, so .table.align-top is defined in the
compat layer rather than assumed to exist. It did not -- Tabler itself has not
been loaded since 0.90.0; those class names are reimplemented in lib/ui.

## The Collections folder cards

`ratio` sizes a box from its aspect ratio with a padding-top pseudo-element;
`h-100` forced height:100% onto the same element. Inside `row g-0` the column is
a stretched flex item, so the two rules disagreed about the height and the
artwork stretched or collapsed depending on how much text the card carried
beside it. That is why the body text never sat level with the top of the image.

Also there: the included-promotion names lived inside the <summary>, where a
long comma list wraps under the disclosure marker and indents every line but
the first. The count stays in the summary, the names moved beneath it.

410 unit tests pass.
