Make the wizard show what it claims to be showing

Four reports, three of them one cause.

Pick your club said "2 selected" and highlighted nothing, and un-ticking
a team left its catalog. /account/teams/:key.json never sent the
`selected` field the picker has always read, so every tile rendered
aria-pressed="false". The CSS highlight keys off that attribute, and so
does the click handler that chooses between create and remove - so
clicking a selected team re-created it and the catalog stayed.

The endpoint now reports the selection using the same rule the remove
route uses, shared as teamPicker.matchesTeam rather than copied, since
the two disagreeing is what this was. Enabled catalogs only: a deselected
team keeps its promotion and fixtures, so it must draw as unpicked or
clicking it to restore it would read as a removal. Selected tiles gain a
tick; a tinted border alone was too quiet across thirty tiles.

Check it works reported a broken install on a working one. Reproduced
live: three empty attempts on UFC Fight Night 287, ONE Fight Night 47 and
Italian Grand Prix Practice 3, all dated 2026-09-05. Newest-per-promotion
then sorted by recency selects one single day, so a thin day takes every
attempt with it, and a Friday practice session is close to the least
uploaded thing in the catalogue. Candidates are now ranked by whether a
release is likely to exist (checkWorthiness, which a promotion implements
for its own session types) and spread over different days, with recency
only breaking ties. Four attempts instead of three.

AEW's preview said "3 events, 29 removed" and listed cards a real refresh
fetches without trouble. Nothing was removed - a preview writes nothing -
but it was not a prediction either: a preview asks the list endpoints
only, skipping the named lookups and the per-round walk, which is where
most of AEW's events come from. It compared a truncated fetch against the
full store and called the difference deletions. Those are now reported as
"Not seen by this preview", with the reason, and no removed count is
shown when the fetch was incomplete. A source with no skippable path
reports removals exactly as before.

526 unit tests plus both standalone verification scripts passing.
