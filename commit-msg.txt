Ask for rutracker's titles, and stop asking for codes nobody uses

rutracker has by far the deepest NFL coverage -- every week of 2025-26 and
2026-27, playoffs included -- and not one query SSS emitted could return any
of it. Its titles are slash-delimited with a DMY date:

  NFL 2026-2027 / Preseason / Week 03 / 28.08.2026 /
    Houston Texans @ Carolina Panthers [Американский футбол, WEB-DL HD/1080p...]

The matcher already accepted that string, Cyrillic and all. The queries were
the problem, in a familiar way: every dated one carried dotted ISO, so on an
ANDing index none could match a title dated 28.08.2026. The same failure as
the nickname releases, one date format along.

Nickname pairs now carry four date tokens -- the stored day and the day before
it, each in dotted ISO and in DMY. DMY for the day before comes first of the
two, because rutracker names by the American local date, which is the day the
UTC timestamp has already rolled past. Both catalogues are reachable inside
the first six queries, which is what a bounded provider list can send.
