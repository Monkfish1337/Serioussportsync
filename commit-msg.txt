NFL, NBA and MLB: measure the gap, then close it

Reported as "very hit and miss ... isn't worth putting out in its current
state", with the real question attached: indexer problem or alias problem?

Measured. One NFL fixture against the live Prowlarr/Usenet stack returned,
among others:

  NFL.Pre.Season.2026.08.28.Arizona.Cardinals.Vs.Green.Bay.Packers.720p...
  NFL.2021.10.28.Cardinals.Vs.Packers.1080p.WEB.h264-SPORTSNET
  NFL.2021.10.28.Packers.at.Cardinals.720p.HDTV.AAC2.0.H264-720pier

Coverage was never the problem. SSS matched the first and rejected the other
two with `no-home-team`: it knew only the full "City Nickname" that ESPN and
the MLB schedule supply, while two of the most prolific groups name releases
by nickname alone. And since every query it emitted carried the full name, an
ANDing index could not have returned them either. Both halves were broken.

Alias tables for all 92 franchises — nickname, city, abbreviation — through
the same preset mechanism the football leagues already use. A bare name that
is ambiguous inside its own league is omitted on purpose: "Chicago" is the
Cubs and the White Sox, "New York" the Mets and the Yankees. The same word
stays where only one team carries it.

Nickname-pair queries: "NFL 2021.10.28 Packers Cardinals". No separator — the
same fixture ships as .Vs. and as .at., so an AND term there halves the reach
for nothing — and always dated, or the pair matches every meeting of those
teams in the index's history. Verified live: that query returns all three
releases above.

One trap closed on the way. The nickname blocklist was written for football
fan names, and four entries collide with American teams: Eagles, Saints, Reds
and Tigers are Crystal Palace, Southampton, Liverpool and Hull, but equally
Philadelphia, New Orleans, Cincinnati and Detroit, where the nickname IS the
release name. Applied globally it would have silently removed the very queries
these tables were added to emit. Now scoped to the presets it was written for,
with the football behaviour pinned by a test.

League prefix plus date stays off where it was measured to lose (169 such
queries returned nothing between them in the last full football run) and is
enabled only for these three, whose releases begin with exactly that pair.

447 tests passing.
